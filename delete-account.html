Delete your Plug & Collect account

Updated on November 8, 2026

In the game (right away)

  1. Open the game, then the My account button at the top of the home screen.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm: it is done right away.

Without the game

Write to malvex.support@gmail.com from your account’s email address, with your nickname. If you sign in with Discord, give your Discord name. Your account is erased within 30 days, and we let you know.

What is erased

Your email, your Discord link, nickname, stars, Ohms, friends, trades, challenge scores, help messages and anti-cheat log. Your cards go back into the boxes, for the other players.

What stays

Nothing that names you. Your progress stays only on your phone, as long as the game is installed there.

See also the Privacy policy page.
