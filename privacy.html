Plug & Collect: privacy policy

Updated on November 14, 2026

Plug & Collect is a game by MALVEX Studios. This page explains what the game keeps about you, why, for how long, and how to erase it all.

What the game keeps

Your settings, progress and age group (under 13, 13-14, or 15 and over) are also kept on your phone. Phone alerts are made on the phone, with no server. Phone motion (for the tilting scenery) never leaves the phone.

What is public

Only your nickname, your scores and your leaderboard places. Your friends also see your cards, to offer trades.

What the game never asks

Your real name, address, age, location, contacts or photos.

Ads

The game may offer ad videos that you choose to watch for a reward, provided by Google AdMob. No ad is ever forced.

In Europe, Google asks for your consent before the first ad. If you agree, Google may use your phone’s advertising ID to choose the ads; if you refuse, the ads are not personalized. Google also sees your IP address and phone type. All ads are for all ages. You can change your mind at any time: Settings → My ad choices. To learn more: how Google uses data.

Where the data is, and who handles it

  • Supabase: the game server, in Ireland (European Union).
  • Google (Gmail): sends the emails with the codes.
  • Discord: only if you choose to sign in with Discord.
  • Google AdMob: only for the ads you choose to watch.

Some of these companies are in the United States; they follow the safeguards required by European law. Your data is never sold.

Legal basis

To run the game you use (performance of the service), and to protect it from cheating (legitimate interest). For ads: your consent, which you can withdraw.

Age

On first launch, the game asks for your year of birth. It keeps only your age group, on your phone: your year is sent neither to our server nor to Google.

  • Under 13: the whole game, but no ads and no online account (no e-mail, no nickname, no friends or leaderboard). Only the invisible player number keeps your game.
  • 13 or 14: ads are not chosen for your tastes (Google does not ask for your consent; French law requires 15). Talk to your parents before creating an account.
  • 15 and over: Google asks for your consent before the first ad.

A mistake? Fix it in the game: Settings → Help & support → Fix my age.

Your rights

You can see, correct, receive or erase your data, and object to its use: write to malvex.support@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Delete your account

In the game: My account → Delete my account. Without the game: see the Delete my account page.
